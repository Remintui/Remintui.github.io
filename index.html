01 · About me

Cloud depth. AI delivery.

I'm Remi Ntui, a technical program and cloud infrastructure leader with 15+ years across enterprise technology, Microsoft Azure, AWS, Linux engineering, security, consulting and training.

For about eight years (2018–2026) at Microsoft I was a Support Escalation Engineer for Azure Linux — leading 2,000+ escalations for Fortune 500 companies, coordinating engineering, networking, identity, security and product teams, and training engineers as an Azure Linux Academy trainer.

Through RemiRose Corporation I design and ship production AI agent systems — including FlowOps AI, an eleven-agent AI workforce for service businesses deployed on AWS. I build daily with agentic tools (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex), run AI agents in CI with automated tests and evaluation gates, and move safety rules out of prompts into fail-closed checks.

My philosophy: treat AI output as a fast first draft, and enforce what matters in code and tests.
